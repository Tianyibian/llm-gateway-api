## ADBE: Priority Research Candidate

Reference close: $250.50. FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance midpoint: $18.145, implying 13.81x earnings if guidance is achieved. This is fiscal-year guidance, not next-twelve-month consensus or an achieved result.

Q3 revenue grew 13%. Operating cash flow was $2.523 billion; subtracting $85 million of property/equipment purchases gives $2.438 billion of simple free cash flow. That cash flow adds back $539 million of stock compensation, which remains an economic cost. Net income increased only about 3.1%, while diluted EPS rose about 10.5%; the lower share count helped. EPS growth should not be confused with equal growth in the underlying business.

Source: [Adobe Q3 FY2026 release, September 10](https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/01906202/au56y4ter.pdf).

Management reported AI-first ARR exceeding $650 million and a December 1 CEO transition. Fast AI-product growth does not by itself establish that AI will benefit the much larger incumbent business. Source: [Adobe prepared remarks](https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/01906202/c6yetrerew.pdf).

Investment hypothesis, not established fact: incumbent workflow integration, enterprise relationships and monetization can remain valuable despite AI competition. Counter-hypothesis: generative tools erode paid seats, pricing and growth faster than Adobe captures new spending.

### Five-Year Sensitivity

All growth rates and exit multiples below are analyst-chosen assumptions, not company guidance or probability-weighted forecasts. No dividends, tax, fees or inflation are modeled. The 15% hurdle is an illustrative research criterion, not an expected or guaranteed return.

Formula: year-five price = $18.145 x (1 + EPS growth)^5 x exit P/E. Annualized return = (year-five price / $250.50)^(1/5) - 1.

| Scenario | Annual EPS growth | Exit P/E | Implied year-five price | Annualized return from reference close |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No growth, lower multiple | 0% | 12x | $217.74 | -2.8% |
| Illustrative middle case | 8% | 16x | $426.58 | 11.2% |
| Faster growth, higher multiple | 14% | 20x | $698.73 | 22.8% |

The middle case supports a maximum entry of about $212.08 for a 15% annualized hurdle. This is NOT a price target, a claim of intrinsic value, or a downside floor. The pessimistic case is not a worst case: earnings contraction or more severe multiple compression would produce greater losses. A changed business outlook requires changing the valuation before reusing this number.

Research triggers: an entry price near/below $212 with the business thesis intact, or fresh operating evidence justifying revised assumptions. Review recurring-revenue growth, paid conversion/retention where disclosed, cash generation after considering compensation, per-share dilution/buybacks, and management execution. Pause the thesis if sustained paid-demand/pricing weakness undermines those assumptions. Do not buy just because the price crosses a threshold.

## MELI: Growth Candidate, Valuation Unresolved

Reference close: $1,839.71. Broker-reported trailing P/E: approximately 49.8x; its earnings basis has not been independently reconciled. This is not directly comparable to Adobe's forward fiscal-year multiple.

Q2 revenue and financial income increased 49.8%, but operating profit declined 17.3%; operating margin was 6.7% versus 12.2% a year earlier. First-half company-defined adjusted free cash flow was $158 million versus $512 million. Operating cash flow includes customer-fund and lending effects, so simply subtracting capital expenditures is not a suitable owner-cash-flow proxy here. The credit book exceeded $16 billion, growing 75%; credit quality and funding matter alongside commerce growth.

Hypothesis: commerce, payments and logistics reinforce customer adoption, while reinvestment temporarily suppresses profits. ## Rules for the $500 Paper Experiment

- Start with $500 simulated cash; no forced allocation or future deposits.
- Use at most two positions, no more than $200 total cost per company, and retain at least $100 cash while building positions. These are experiment limits, not loss guarantees or optimal allocations.
- First simulated entry: up to $100 only after a written business thesis, conservative valuation and failure criteria all pass. For ADBE, the current $212 screening level is one conditional input, not a standing order.
- Second simulated entry: at most $100 after new evidence supports the thesis and valuation still qualifies. A lower share price alone is insufficient.
- Record every decision prospectively, including decisions to wait. Compare with a same-start-date $500 QQQM benchmark, including idle cash and execution costs. Do not optimize by rewriting past decisions.
- Review after material filings or business news. This document creates no scheduled monitor and no unattended service.
- If the thesis fails, reconsider holding even at a loss. Distinguish temporary price volatility from permanent damage to earnings power.
