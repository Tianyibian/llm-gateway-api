Orders and Invoices

How do I update my billing information?

Changing billing information before payment capture.

This article explains common order-management tasks such as invoices, cancellations, changes, and order status. Some changes are only available before the order enters fulfillment.

Changing billing information before payment capture. The guidance below is written for customers who purchased directly from AsterHome Smart Appliances or from an authorized channel that uses AsterHome support workflows. If your item was purchased through another marketplace, reseller, installer, property manager, or service provider, some steps may differ because that seller may control payment, shipping, or account records.

Good to know: Order actions may disappear after an order enters fulfillment. If an option is not available in your account, contact support for review.

Before you begin

Have your order number, product model, serial number if available, account email address, and any relevant photos or screenshots ready. For smart appliances, also make sure you can access the AsterHome app and the email account used at purchase. If the article relates to a connected product, remove personal information or shared users when appropriate.

Order confirmation emails are sent shortly after checkout. If you do not receive one, check spam folders and confirm that the email address on the order is correct.

Step-by-step guidance

  1. Sign in to your account or use the guest order lookup link.
  2. Open the order details page.
  3. Review available actions such as cancel, reorder, invoice, or contact support.
  4. Submit the request before fulfillment deadlines where applicable.
  5. Save confirmation emails for your records.

Business, property management, and installer orders may require additional verification, purchase documentation, or shipping coordination.

What to expect next

After you complete the steps, AsterHome may send an email confirmation, update your account page, or ask for additional information. Keep all confirmation numbers, carrier tracking numbers, photos, and support case messages until the matter is resolved. If a request requires review by another team, customer support will use the information you provided to determine the next available action.

Common reasons support may need more information

  • The order cannot be matched to the account email or order number.
  • The item is part of a bundle, subscription, warranty claim, or installation appointment.
  • The product has missing accessories, unclear condition, or incomplete device reset status.
  • The request involves a high-value item, delivery exception, payment dispute, or possible account security concern.

When to contact us

Contact AsterHome Support if the self-service option is not available, the information shown in your account looks incorrect, or the issue affects safety, privacy, account security, a delivery exception, or a high-value product. Include the steps you already tried so the support team can avoid repeating basic checks.
