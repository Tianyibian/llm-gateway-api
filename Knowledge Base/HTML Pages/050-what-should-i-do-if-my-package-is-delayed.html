Shipping and Delivery

What should I do if my package is delayed?

Steps to follow when tracking has not updated.

This article explains shipping and delivery options for smart appliances, accessories, and connected devices. Delivery timing may vary by product type, address, carrier capacity, and fulfillment availability.

Steps to follow when tracking has not updated. The guidance below is written for customers who purchased directly from AsterHome Smart Appliances or from an authorized channel that uses AsterHome support workflows. If your item was purchased through another marketplace, reseller, installer, property manager, or service provider, some steps may differ because that seller may control payment, shipping, or account records.

Good to know: Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Weather, carrier constraints, address issues, high-volume periods, or freight appointment availability may affect the final delivery date.

Before you begin

Have your order number, product model, serial number if available, account email address, and any relevant photos or screenshots ready. For smart appliances, also make sure you can access the AsterHome app and the email account used at purchase. If the article relates to a connected product, remove personal information or shared users when appropriate.

For high-value or large items, keep an eye on appointment emails, freight calls, or signature requirements. Missing an appointment may create a redelivery delay or carrier fee.

Step-by-step guidance

  1. Open your order status page.
  2. Review the shipping method, tracking number, and delivery address.
  3. Check carrier updates for scans, delays, or delivery appointments.
  4. Inspect the package when it arrives and keep the packaging until you confirm the item is complete.
  5. Contact support if tracking is inconsistent or the package appears lost or damaged.

Inspect the outside of the package at delivery. If the packaging appears crushed, wet, punctured, or opened, take photos before unpacking and keep all materials until support confirms next steps.

What to expect next

After you complete the steps, AsterHome may send an email confirmation, update your account page, or ask for additional information. Keep all confirmation numbers, carrier tracking numbers, photos, and support case messages until the matter is resolved. If a request requires review by another team, customer support will use the information you provided to determine the next available action.

Common reasons support may need more information

  • The order cannot be matched to the account email or order number.
  • The item is part of a bundle, subscription, warranty claim, or installation appointment.
  • The product has missing accessories, unclear condition, or incomplete device reset status.
  • The request involves a high-value item, delivery exception, payment dispute, or possible account security concern.

When to contact us

Contact AsterHome Support if the self-service option is not available, the information shown in your account looks incorrect, or the issue affects safety, privacy, account security, a delivery exception, or a high-value product. Include the steps you already tried so the support team can avoid repeating basic checks.
