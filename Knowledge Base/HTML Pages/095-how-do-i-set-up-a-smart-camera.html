How do I set up a smart camera?

Camera setup, mounting, and privacy guidance.

This article provides general setup guidance for smart home devices. Always follow the printed safety instructions included with your product and use a qualified professional when required by electrical, HVAC, or building codes.

Camera setup, mounting, and privacy guidance. The guidance below is written for customers who purchased directly from AsterHome Smart Appliances or from an authorized channel that uses AsterHome support workflows. If your item was purchased through another marketplace, reseller, installer, property manager, or service provider, some steps may differ because that seller may control payment, shipping, or account records.

Good to know: For hardwired, HVAC-connected, door lock, or mounted security products, follow local rules and consider professional installation.

Before you begin

Have your order number, product model, serial number if available, account email address, and any relevant photos or screenshots ready. For smart appliances, also make sure you can access the AsterHome app and the email account used at purchase. If the article relates to a connected product, remove personal information or shared users when appropriate.

During setup, keep your phone, app, and device close together. Some products use Bluetooth for discovery before switching to Wi-Fi.

Step-by-step guidance

  1. Unbox the product and confirm all required parts are included.
  2. Download or open the AsterHome app.
  3. Place the device near power and a reliable 2.4 GHz or compatible Wi-Fi network during setup.
  4. Follow the in-app pairing steps and name the device clearly.
  5. Test the device before mounting, scheduling, or sharing access.

Give each device a clear room-based name, such as Kitchen Smart Plug or Front Door Camera, so automations and support cases are easier to manage.

What to expect next

After you complete the steps, AsterHome may send an email confirmation, update your account page, or ask for additional information. Keep all confirmation numbers, carrier tracking numbers, photos, and support case messages until the matter is resolved. If a request requires review by another team, customer support will use the information you provided to determine the next available action.

Common reasons support may need more information

  • The order cannot be matched to the account email or order number.
  • The item is part of a bundle, subscription, warranty claim, or installation appointment.
  • The product has missing accessories, unclear condition, or incomplete device reset status.
  • The request involves a high-value item, delivery exception, payment dispute, or possible account security concern.

When to contact us

Contact AsterHome Support if the self-service option is not available, the information shown in your account looks incorrect, or the issue affects safety, privacy, account security, a delivery exception, or a high-value product. Include the steps you already tried so the support team can avoid repeating basic checks.
