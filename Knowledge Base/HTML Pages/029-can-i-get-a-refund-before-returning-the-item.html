Refunds

Can I get a refund before returning the item?

When advance refunds may or may not be available.

This article explains how refunds are reviewed, processed, and returned to the original payment method or another approved option. Refund timing may depend on inspection, payment provider rules, and order status.

When advance refunds may or may not be available. The guidance below is written for customers who purchased directly from AsterHome Smart Appliances or from an authorized channel that uses AsterHome support workflows. If your item was purchased through another marketplace, reseller, installer, property manager, or service provider, some steps may differ because that seller may control payment, shipping, or account records.

Good to know: Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method unless store credit or another approved option applies. Your bank may need additional time to post the credit.

Before you begin

Have your order number, product model, serial number if available, account email address, and any relevant photos or screenshots ready. For smart appliances, also make sure you can access the AsterHome app and the email account used at purchase. If the article relates to a connected product, remove personal information or shared users when appropriate.

Refund approval and refund posting are separate events. Approval means AsterHome has authorized the refund. Posting means your bank, card issuer, wallet, or payment provider has placed the funds back in your account.

Step-by-step guidance

  1. Open your order or support case.
  2. Confirm that the return, cancellation, price adjustment, or service issue is eligible.
  3. Provide the requested payment, order, and product information.
  4. Wait for inspection or internal approval when required.
  5. Check your email and account for refund status updates.

If the refund relates to a returned product, the return must usually be scanned by the carrier and inspected before the refund can be completed.

What to expect next

After you complete the steps, AsterHome may send an email confirmation, update your account page, or ask for additional information. Keep all confirmation numbers, carrier tracking numbers, photos, and support case messages until the matter is resolved. If a request requires review by another team, customer support will use the information you provided to determine the next available action.

Common reasons support may need more information

  • The order cannot be matched to the account email or order number.
  • The item is part of a bundle, subscription, warranty claim, or installation appointment.
  • The product has missing accessories, unclear condition, or incomplete device reset status.
  • The request involves a high-value item, delivery exception, payment dispute, or possible account security concern.

When to contact us

Contact AsterHome Support if the self-service option is not available, the information shown in your account looks incorrect, or the issue affects safety, privacy, account security, a delivery exception, or a high-value product. Include the steps you already tried so the support team can avoid repeating basic checks.
