Can I return a smart doorbell after mounting it?

Return rules for mounted smart doorbells.

This article explains customer-facing return guidance for smart home products purchased from AsterHome. It is intended to help you understand eligibility, preparation steps, and what to expect after submitting a request.

Return rules for mounted smart doorbells. The guidance below is written for customers who purchased directly from AsterHome Smart Appliances or from an authorized channel that uses AsterHome support workflows. If your item was purchased through another marketplace, reseller, installer, property manager, or service provider, some steps may differ because that seller may control payment, shipping, or account records.

Good to know: A return may be delayed or denied if the product is missing required parts, shows signs of misuse, contains customer data, or is returned outside the approved window.

Before you begin

Have your order number, product model, serial number if available, account email address, and any relevant photos or screenshots ready. For smart appliances, also make sure you can access the AsterHome app and the email account used at purchase. If the article relates to a connected product, remove personal information or shared users when appropriate.

Before sending a connected device back, remove it from your AsterHome account and perform a factory reset when the instructions recommend it. This protects your privacy and helps the inspection team process the item faster.

Step-by-step guidance

  1. Sign in and open your order history.
  2. Select the item and choose Start Return.
  3. Answer the eligibility questions honestly and upload photos if requested.
  4. Pack the item with all included parts and remove personal data from connected devices.
  5. Ship the package using the approved label or follow the instructions shown in the portal.

If you purchased a kit, return all required kit components together. A missing hub, remote, dock, mounting plate, power adapter, sensor, cable, or accessory may reduce the refund or require a support review.

What to expect next

After you complete the steps, AsterHome may send an email confirmation, update your account page, or ask for additional information. Keep all confirmation numbers, carrier tracking numbers, photos, and support case messages until the matter is resolved. If a request requires review by another team, customer support will use the information you provided to determine the next available action.

Common reasons support may need more information

  • The order cannot be matched to the account email or order number.
  • The item is part of a bundle, subscription, warranty claim, or installation appointment.
  • The product has missing accessories, unclear condition, or incomplete device reset status.
  • The request involves a high-value item, delivery exception, payment dispute, or possible account security concern.

When to contact us

Contact AsterHome Support if the self-service option is not available, the information shown in your account looks incorrect, or the issue affects safety, privacy, account security, a delivery exception, or a high-value product. Include the steps you already tried so the support team can avoid repeating basic checks.
